Secret Room

Two people, one shared password. Messages are encrypted in your browser, travel directly between the two of you, and vanish after the time you pick, up to 75 seconds.

What this protects, and what it doesn't

Protects against

  • Someone opening this page without the password.
  • The relay and STUN operators reading what you write: they only ever see encrypted setup data.
  • Anyone impersonating the other person without the password, because the connection fingerprint is sealed with it.

Does not protect against

  • Metadata. The relay sees that a room is being joined, and when. STUN sees your IP address.
  • Your two IP addresses are visible to each other in a direct connection.
  • A weak or guessable password. It can be cracked offline.
  • Screenshots, or a camera pointed at the screen.
  • Spyware on either device. The key sits in memory while the page is open.
  • Whoever controls the website host changing the code. Verify or self-host the page.
  • Relay fallback. When a direct link is impossible (strict firewalls), Automatic sends the same sealed messages through the relay instead. Content stays unreadable, but the relay then sees every message's timing and size, and public relays may rate-limit free use.
Waiting for the other person

Manual pairing. One person presses "Create invite" and sends the code over any channel. The other pastes it here, presses "Use pasted code", and sends back the reply code. Then the first person pastes that reply and presses the same button. Codes are encrypted with your password and expire after 10 minutes.

Quiet in here. Messages vanish on their own.

Screenshots, spyware and weak passwords are not covered. Timing and message size are visible on the network.
