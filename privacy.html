Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 November 2026

Short version: this project has no server, no accounts, no cookies and no analytics. Your password and messages stay in your browser and are encrypted before they leave it. The free public services it uses to connect people can see technical details such as your IP address, but not what you write.

What we collect

Nothing. The project is a set of static pages with no backend that we run. We do not receive, store or log your password, messages, files, searches or IP address. We cannot read your chats or recover a lost password.

What stays on your device

  • Your password, the keys derived from it and your messages exist only in the page's memory. Locking, closing the tab, 5 minutes of inactivity or 2 minutes in the background wipes them.
  • Messages disappear after the time you pick (5 to 75 seconds).
  • Optional TURN details you type (Turnix token, Xirsys identity, secret and channel) are used once to request temporary credentials, are never saved, and are cleared when you join or lock.
  • The pages use no cookies, local storage, analytics or advertising. On the search page, your search terms appear in the page address in your own browser.

Third-party services involved

The pages talk directly from your browser to public services we do not control. Each can see the technical details of your connection (at least your IP address and the time), and is governed by its own policy.

In a direct link the two people can see each other's IP address, because that is how a direct connection works. In the group chat every member connects directly to every other member, so all members of a room can see each other's IP address. Share the group password only with people you trust.

What this does not protect against

Metadata and timing, IP addresses, weak or shared passwords, screenshots, malware on your device, and a compromised hosting account that swaps the code. Use four or more random words as the password.

Children

This project is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect any personal data from anyone.
